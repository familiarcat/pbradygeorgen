- **Global Access**: Works across all Cursor instances and workspaces
- **Natural Language**: Responds to phrases like "activate n8n" and "all hands on deck"
- **Star Trek Authenticity**: True to character skills and personalities
- **n8n Integration**: Connects to n8n instances for workflow management
- **Observation Lounge**: Full crew consultation and synthesis
